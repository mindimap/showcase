## What is MindiMap?

MindiMap is a browser-based AI diagramming and visual thinking suite. It combines **Mind Maps**, **Flowcharts**, **Task Management**, and **Gantt Charts** into a single, seamless workspace. Unlike traditional tools, MindiMap features an integrated AI assistant that generates complete project structures—from nodes and branches to detailed timeline dependencies—from a single text prompt. It requires zero installation, works offline, and is the world's first visual tool with native, full **RTL (Right-to-Left)** support for Persian and Arabic.

## The Core Tools

### 🧠 Mind Maps  
Radial or tree layouts perfect for brainstorming, project planning, and taking notes. AI can generate a full hierarchy from a single prompt.

### 🔀 Flowcharts  
Design algorithms and processes visually with 8 shape types, arrowheads, yes/no labels, loop-backs, and merge nodes.

### ☑ Task Management  
Visual checklists where every node is a task with a checkbox and a live progress bar.

### 📅 Gantt Charts  
Full project timelines with milestones and dependencies. Instantly convert your mind map into a timeline using AI. Perfect for company reporting and thesis presentations.

### 🎤 Speech to Mindmap  
Describe your idea out loud. MindiMap listens, transcribes your voice, and builds the map while you talk — no typing needed.

### 📄 PDF to Mindmap  
Upload a research paper and MindiMap extracts the abstract, methodology, results, and conclusion into a structured map.

## 14 Ready-Made Templates

Start from a **Research Paper** outline (full academic structure: title, abstract, introduction, literature review, methodology, results, discussion, references), a **PRISMA 2020** systematic-review flowchart, or any of these: Project plan, Study plan, SWOT analysis, Content calendar, Product roadmap, Weekly tasks, Sprint planning, Blog outline, OKRs, Client onboarding, Travel plan, Task list.
