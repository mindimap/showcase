## Core Tools

### 🧠 Mind Map Maker
Create radial or tree-layout [mind maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_map) for brainstorming, project planning, and note-taking. The AI generates a full hierarchy from one sentence.

### 🔀 Flowchart Software
Design algorithms and processes with 8 shape types, arrowheads, yes/no labels, loop-backs, and merge nodes. Export as SVG or PNG.

### ☑ Task Management
Visual task trees where every node has a checkbox and a live progress bar. Filter by done or open to see what is left.

### 📅 Gantt Chart Generator
Build full project timelines with milestones and dependencies. Or describe your project in one sentence and let AI produce the entire Gantt chart. Perfect for company reporting, thesis presentations, and construction project plans.

### 🎤 Speech to Mindmap
Describe your idea out loud. MindiMap listens, transcribes your voice, and builds the map while you talk — no typing needed. Works in all 9 supported languages.

### 📄 PDF to Mindmap
Upload a research paper and MindiMap extracts the abstract, methodology, results, and conclusion into a structured [mind map](https://mindimap.com/blog/what-is-a-mind-map/).
